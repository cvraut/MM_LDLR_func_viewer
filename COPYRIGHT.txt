Genome data
-----------

genome/chrom.sizes and genome/hg38_ldlr_region.fa are derived from the
GRCh38/hg38 human reference assembly, obtained from the UCSC Genome Browser
(https://genome.ucsc.edu). genome/demo_sequence.fa is randomly generated
sequence, not real genome data.
