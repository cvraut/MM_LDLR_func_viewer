Reference bases from the UCSC Genome Browser, assembly hg38 (GRCh38). The sequence represents the human reference genome; it does not show variants present in any individual or cell line. The exact region included is listed in the box above.

Zoom with the slider at the top right, alt/option + scroll over the tracks, or by dragging across a region. Hover any base to see its exact position.

- Positions are 1-based, matching the UCSC browser: the first base of chromosome 19 is `chr19:1`.
- Bases are shown on the forward (+) strand. For a gene on the − strand, read the reverse complement.
- All bases are shown in uppercase
- If bases are not shown, that data was not included in this locus to keep the webpage small and responsive.
